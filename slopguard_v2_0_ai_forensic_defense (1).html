Why AI vs. Human? Understanding Forensic Indications

Ever wondered how SlopGuard distinguishes genuine human mastery from synthetic diffusion slop? Explore the key physical, optical, spectral, and anatomical markers below to learn and spot the differences yourself.

Authentic Human Markers

Physical laws, brushwork dynamics & camera optics

  • Brush Stroke Velocity & Impasto Layering

    Human physical painting shows natural pressure variance, directional paint build-up (impasto), and subtle micro-imperfections in stroke termination.

  • Anatomical & Structural Symmetry Continuity

    Human artists maintain logical spatial continuity: background lines pass correctly behind foreground objects, and hands follow realistic bone physics.

  • Vector Lighting & Optical Reflections

    Light cast across clothing folds, eye catchlights, and cast shadows strictly follows true 3D ray-tracing physics with matching focal depth.

  • Cryptographic Provenance & EXIF Lens Data

    Contains valid camera sensor ISO noise grids, lens aperture profiles, and C2PA content credentials stamped at creation.

Synthetic AI Slop Markers

Diffusion noise grids, waxy textures & spatial glitches

  • Fourier Latent Frequency Artifacts

    Latent diffusion models (Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, DALL-E) leave telltale mathematical Gaussian frequency spikes in high-pass Fourier transformations.

  • Anatomic Distortion & Asymmetric Glitches

    Fused fingers, non-matching earring designs, irregular pupils, floating hair strands, and nonsensical background text/architecture.

  • Waxy Skin Over-Smoothing & Plastic Sheen

    Absence of true skin pore micro-distribution; replaced by uniform plastic smoothness coupled with unnatural hyper-detail in non-focal zones.

  • Stripped Metadata & Missing C2PA Header

    Lack of camera EXIF headers or presence of generator parameters residue in PNG metadata chunks.
